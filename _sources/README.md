# Cosmic Microwave Background

A [Learn Astropy](https://learn.astropy.org) tutorial series on the cosmic microwave background (CMB):

1. **Measure the temperature of the Universe from its blackbody spectrum** — fit a blackbody to the COBE/FIRAS spectrum.
2. **Explore an all-sky map of the cosmic microwave background** — load, plot, and characterize a *Planck* CMB map.
3. **Measure the age of the Universe with a standard ruler** — stack hot spots, measure their angular size, and estimate the age of the Universe with `astropy.cosmology`.
